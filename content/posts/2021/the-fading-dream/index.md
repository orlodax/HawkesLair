I want a story, built memories\
accumulated places, smiles, sun streams through lashes\
be allowed to cherish\
be cherished in return.\
I feel, I feel all the time\
it's not just melancholy\
invisible strings tying me to all\
tugging at my heart\
an itch I cannot scratch\
a silent music,\
a sunset unobserved.\
A leaning tree,\
is his bending fatigue or comfort?\
Drink at my shadow, sleep at my feet,\
lend me your dreams, of them I'll take good care,\
I will tuck you in with them, with a sparkle of my own.\
Pink flowers blossoming down on your music,\
warm air sweetly swirling,\
bending, welcoming.\
Give me back my time, my story, my life, my youth, my vigor,\
all of which, everything and yet more again,\
meant to be surrendered, to you.
