[Wikipedia (it):](http://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connettivismo_%28letteratura%29)

*"The term **nexialism** was introduced in science fiction by [Alfred Elton van Vogt](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._E._van_Vogt), a canadian writer, in a series of tales gathered together in the book [The Voyage of the Space Beagle,](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Voyage_of_the_Space_Beagle) to describe a fictional doctrine. Van Vogt's theory assumes: since each discipline reached the highest specialization levels, a new science was needed to re-establish the connections between the various competences and knowledges. Who performs this task is defined nexialist."\*

[Moreover:\](http://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connettivismo_%28teoria_dell%27apprendimento%29)*"In an attempt to explain a new learning modality, based on network paradigm, a new learning theory in the digital era emerged lately, named **nexialism.** \*

*...\*

*A peculiar aspect of nexialism is the use of the network with nodes and connections as the central metaphor to explain how learning process occurs. In this metaphor, a node is whatever may be connected to another node: informations, datas, images, feelings. To learn is a connections-creating process and develops a network."*

This last theory of them I like, since it's **analog** to the structure that in man performs that function: the brain (network made of neurons and synapsis... if you want to take a deeper look on the subject: [Neural networks](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network))
