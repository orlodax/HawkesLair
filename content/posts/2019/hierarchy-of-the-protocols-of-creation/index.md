Some premises:

— the physical universe is entirely constituted, ultimately, by energy (matter E = mc², dark matter, various forms of manifestation of energy, dark energy). Moreover, matter is mostly empty space, from this we observe that the solidity and materiality that can be experienced with the senses are actually an illusion. I conclude that the universe is more like software than anything else: information encoded and conveyed by energy. The source of all energy is Jehovah, the great architect of everything that exists and becomes.

— Jehovah God is the Eternal, has neither end nor beginning. We have read the illustration about the lines with positive and negative numbers saying that the existence of God extends to infinity in both directions, but this does not satisfy us fully. Even if we cannot completely understand certain things, on the book *All Scripture* we find something more. Among many interesting things, I quote: *"Jehovah himself lives in eternity.* *As for his creatures, he was pleased to place them over time.* *The angels of heaven, even the rebellious Satan, are fully aware of the passage of time. "* That is, for all God's creatures, the **temporal protocol** is valid, He alone is exempt. Since time itself is defined as the consequence of change, or the fruit of becoming, and that the meaning of the name of God has directly to do with it, we could imagine time as a consequence of God's actions, or as something that He emanates.

— space and time are essential between them. Curiously, even if we take them for granted, the very definitions of what [time is](https://translate.googleusercontent.com/translate_c?depth=1&rurl=translate.google.com&sl=it&sp=nmt4&tl=en&u=http://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempo&xid=17259,15700023,15700186,15700190,15700256,15700259,15700262,15700265,15700271,15700283&usg=ALkJrhj90gcER4WloL5OQU4jJHyKGJ-TVg "Time") and what [space is](https://translate.googleusercontent.com/translate_c?depth=1&rurl=translate.google.com&sl=it&sp=nmt4&tl=en&u=http://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spazio_%2528fisica%2529&xid=17259,15700023,15700186,15700190,15700256,15700259,15700262,15700265,15700271,15700283&usg=ALkJrhhN_Ycb6SDVteIBT3j9WmuGKEWDzw "Space") (see encyclopedias) are not univocal and absolute, but relative and multiple (and unsatisfactory). Evidently, as in the case of energy (of which we can only observe the effects), it can be an infrastructure, a protocol, so fundamental in the nature of the universe, that we can only experience some aspects of it. I will call it a **space-time protocol** , as the experimental evidence so far apparently shows that space and time are one.

-

Now I am not a physicist, nor an astrophysicist, nor something similar, but I wonder: is it possible that this space-time protocol is not limited to the physical universe? Could it not be something, a larger infrastructure *within which* our universe is located? This would at least explain how spiritual creatures other than God are subject to time and interact in "our" spacetime in certain ways. That is, time could have begun with the first creation of God, which is not the Big Bang but the creation of Jesus as the first-born son.

And, given that time and space are part of the same protocol, we could imagine a sort of spiritual space, indeed this is necessary: ​​obviously not a three-dimensional geometric space. If it is true that the protocol is *space-time it* is also true that it is not a physical object or something tangible, but rather a set of rules or laws within which the events of that "place" occur (place not intended as a space; the example of software returns: a network protocol is not something solid, but a **system** that manages traffic and data processing within the site, the network, in which it operates). And many events occur in the spiritual realm, and they must have an unimaginable complexity for us ...

And all this leads me to think that the Protocols of Creation operate in a cascade (see [OSI](https://translate.googleusercontent.com/translate_c?depth=1&rurl=translate.google.com&sl=it&sp=nmt4&tl=en&u=http://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Systems_Interconnection&xid=17259,15700023,15700186,15700190,15700256,15700259,15700262,15700265,15700271,15700283&usg=ALkJrhievzt5Klnppq_RXCRILNfLIwhI_Q "Protocol hierarchy") ); that from every level one can freely access all the lower ones, but none of the superiors. Similarly, that every valid protocol in a level is valid in all the underlying ones, but not necessarily in the above ones. Examples: time (or spacetime) is valid for all but one, and matter applies to the physical universe but not to the spiritual one ...

What do you think?
