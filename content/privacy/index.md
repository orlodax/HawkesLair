This is a static site: HTML pages served as they are. There is no database, no
forms, and no code running on the server.

## What I don't collect

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no newsletter, no contact form. I
neither receive nor store anything about who visits this site.

## What happens anyway

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like any web server, GitHub records the
requests it receives — IP address, page requested, browser — to keep the
service running and protect it from abuse. Those logs belong to GitHub, not to
me: I have no access to them. Their terms are in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Some posts embed YouTube videos or images hosted elsewhere. When you open one
of those posts, the service in question sees the request exactly as if you had
visited it directly.

## Comments

The comments at the foot of some posts are a **static archive**: text carried
over from this blog's previous WordPress incarnation, frozen at the point of
migration. It is not a live comment system and nothing new can be added. They
carry the names people chose at the time; if you are one of them and would
rather your name were removed, write to me and I'll take it out.

## Contact

For anything concerning this page or your data, open an issue on the
[site's repository](https://github.com/orlodax/HawkesLair).
